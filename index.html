About Us

Our Story Since 1996

The Coffee Hub began in 1996 with a small team and a simple idea: buy coffee directly from local farmers, pay them fairly, and share the best of Kenyan coffee with more people. In those early years we worked with just a handful of growers.

As word spread, more farmers joined us and our customers grew with them. Over the years we built our own processing facility and began supplying cafés, restaurants and homes across Kenya and East Africa.

Today we partner with farming families across the country, and we still believe what we started with: great coffee begins with the people who grow it.

What We Do

From the Farm to Your Cup

Direct from Local Farmers

We buy our coffee directly from local farmers across Kenya, paying fair prices for every harvest. By working straight with the growers, we support farming families and help local businesses grow alongside us.

Coffee Processing

Our modern processing facilities keep every stage streamlined and efficient, from sorting and drying to grading and packing. Careful quality checks along the way make sure every batch keeps its rich flavour and aroma.

Coffee Distribution

We are the exclusive coffee supplier to many of the most popular cafés and restaurants in Kenya. When you order a cup at one of the country's favourite spots, there is a good chance it started with us.
